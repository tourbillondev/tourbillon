Tourbillon Alerts: what each post means (part 2 of 2)

OPEN BOOK AND AUCTION
🔴 buyback-failed: a buyback from a maker failed; the pool paid the seller and now carries that raw. Who: admin.
🔴 matched-uncovered: a matched source was flagged and its raw now counts in the pool's stress. Who: admin.
🟡 buyback-shortfall: a buyback fell short beyond rounding; slashed from the source, then absorbed by the pool. Who: admin.
🟡 vault-slashed (information): a maker's vault was slashed (a shortfall or a forfeited bond).
🟡 auction-started / auction-forfeit: a weekly risk auction lot opened, or a bond went to the pool. Who: makers bid.
🟡 auction-settled (information): a lot settled, with or without a winner.
🟡 auction-settle-due: a lot's reveal ended an hour ago, unsettled. Who: keeper operator (anyone can settle).

GOVERNANCE
🟡 timelock-queued: an admin action was queued, with the contract, the call and when it can run. Who: everyone reviews; the Safe can cancel it.
🟡 timelock-executed / timelock-cancelled (information): a queued action ran, or was cancelled.
🔴 role-changed: a role was granted or revoked on a governed contract. Who: admin confirms it was announced.
🟡 paused: opens, a market or deposits were paused, and by whom. Who: the Safe reviews and unpauses when satisfied.
🟢 unpaused (information): a pause was lifted, and by whom.
🟡 monitor-error: the risk-monitor's own check failed. Who: risk-monitor operator.

RELEASES (staff group)
✅ SHIPPED AND RELEASED · Update NN: deployed, checks passed, public copy and GitHub release out.
🟡 RELEASE DELAYED · Update NN: live; the public copy and GitHub release are retrying, then SHIPPED AND RELEASED.
🟡 HELD · Update NN: a pre-release check failed; nothing deployed; the next update stays on time. Who: release owner.
🔴 ROLLED BACK · Update NN: deployed, a post-deploy check failed, the previous version is back. Who: release owner.
